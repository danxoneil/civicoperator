The opportunities that fit you — verified and delivered every morning, with who to call. Your State Fit Brief tells you where you fit once; this keeps that answer current.

The opposite of a firehose

Anyone can scrape fifty state websites. The result is noise you still have to read, dedupe, and vet.

The firehose problem

Raw trackers push dozens of links a day — most irrelevant to you, none vetted. You still have to open each one and decide whether it's real and whether it fits. That's analyst time you don't have.

The point-in-time problem

A one-time brief is the right way to find your fit — but the program moves. New NOFOs, RFPs, awards, and bills land every week, and a snapshot goes stale the fiscal quarter after you buy it.

What this is instead

The two or three things that fit your organization, checked by a human before they reach you, with the contacts and deadlines you need to act the same day. Judgment, not a dashboard.

What lands in your inbox each morning

A single, scannable digest — not per-alert spam. Every opportunity carries four things.

01

What it is

The procurement, award, or legislative action, in plain language, with a link to the source document.

02

Why it fits you

The specific funded priorities and dollars in your rubric it maps to — so you see the fit, not just the item.

03

The clock

Deadlines and where it sits in the state's timeline, so you know what's urgent and what can wait.

04

Who to call

The relevant state contacts and the organizations already active on that topic, prioritized — so you're not starting from a blank page.

One digest each morning. An occasional Friday text when something genuinely can't wait for Monday.

What "verified" means

This is the difference between an alert service and an intelligence service.

A human checks it before you see it

Matching runs automatically across every state's output — but nothing reaches you until I've read it and stand behind it. You don't get "probably relevant." You get opportunities that have been checked against your rubric by a person. That verification step is the entire product: it's why a paying client can act on the digest without re-doing the work.

Tuned to your rubric — and it stays yours

One private board, set to your criteria, that follows your strategy as it changes.

Your criteria drive the match

We set up a private board with your fit criteria — your states, your topics, the kind of work you win. You edit it; the matcher follows it. As your strategy shifts, your digest shifts with it. No change orders, no waiting on me.

A running history you can search

Every opportunity you've ever been sent stays on your board as a searchable record — connected back to the master intelligence — so the pipeline you've been building doesn't vanish into old email.
