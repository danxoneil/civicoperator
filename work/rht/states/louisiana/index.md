## Questions & answers

What is Louisiana's Rural Health Transformation Program allocation, and how is the funding structured?

Louisiana drew **$208,374,448** in Year-1 funding under the CMS Rural Health Transformation Program — a top-five national award — and is positioned to pull down roughly $1.4 billion across the program's five years. It is Louisiana's share of the federal $50 billion program, with Year-1 dollars released through a fast-moving series of Notices of Funding Opportunity funded via Cooperative Endeavor Agreements.

Which agency administers Louisiana's RHTP, and who leads it?

The [Louisiana Department of Health (LDH)](https://ldh.la.gov/page/rural-health-transformation-program) administers the program through its new **Office of Rural Health Transformation and Sustainability (ORHTS)**, which absorbed the former State Office of Rural Health. Program inquiries go to [RuralHealthTransformation@la.gov](mailto:RuralHealthTransformation@la.gov).

What is Louisiana's rural health transformation plan trying to accomplish?

Louisiana's plan is built around numbered initiatives spanning workforce, innovative and collaborative care, telehealth/access (MRAHA), and sustainable access to care. A centerpiece is a data-driven rural Medicaid **Alternative Payment Model**: working from a Milliman review of Medicaid claims, LDH found rural parishes run higher per-member cost but lower inpatient and readmission rates — a pattern it reads as a physical-access problem, not overutilization — and is funding a primary-care-led, provider-owned APM in response. Key project tags include Value-Based Shift, Clinical Pipeline and Rural EMS Tech.

[Louisiana RHTP hub →](https://ldh.la.gov/page/rural-health-transformation-program)

Where is the money going — which initiatives is Louisiana funding, and at what share?

- **Rural Health Facilities Capital Improvement** (Initiative 5, Sustainable Access) — $39.9M, the largest opportunity, prioritizing ready-to-implement projects; awards $100,000–$10M.
- **Rural Medicaid Alternative Payment Model** (Innovative Care) — roughly $30M Year 1.
- **Telehealth Infrastructure for Rural Access** (Initiative 4, MRAHA) — $5M, awards $50,000–$500,000.
- **Rural Clinician Credit Bank** (Initiative 1, Workforce) — $5.63M.
- Plus **Collaborative Provider Models**, **Food is Medicine**, **Regional Care Conveners**, and the vendor-facing **Rural Tech Catalyst Fund**.

The full breakdown is in Louisiana's budget narrative.

What has Louisiana procured so far, and what's open right now?

Louisiana ran an unusually dense NOFO calendar and is now moving from solicitation into award. The **Rural Clinician Credit Bank** ($5.63M) closed June 25; **Telehealth Infrastructure** ($5M) and **Capital Improvement** ($39.9M) closed July 10; **Collaborative Provider Models** closed August 5; the **Rural Medicaid APM** (~$30M) closed August 7; and **Food is Medicine** and **Regional Care Conveners** closed August 14. As of late July, LDH's page showed several programs as Closed with **Notices of Intent to Contract** slated for late July through mid-August, and Rural Tech Catalyst Fund Cohort 1 awardees expected mid-September. The running record is in the dispatches below.

What are the key upcoming dates and deadlines in Louisiana?

Most Year-1 application windows have now closed (deadlines ran July 10, August 5, August 7 and August 14, 2026), and the state is in its award phase — watch for **Notice of Intent to Contract** announcements across late July into mid-August and RTCF Cohort 1 awards in mid-September. LDH holds a monthly RHTP webinar (the next was August 6). Upcoming deadlines, webinars and vendor showcases surface first in the dispatches below and on the funding-opportunities page.

[LA RHTP funding opportunities →](https://ldh.la.gov/page/rhtp-funding-opportunities)

What legislation or state action enables Louisiana's RHTP?

The Rural Health Transformation Program was created by **Section 71401 of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (2025)** and is administered federally by CMS. In Louisiana, Gov. **Jeff Landry** signed an executive order creating the Office of Rural Health Transformation and Sustainability inside LDH and a RHTP Advisory Council that seats the chairs of the House and Senate Health and Welfare committees. The legislature added a standing oversight hook via **Senate Resolution 198** (passed 36-0 on June 1, 2026), directing LDH to report to the legislature on its implementation of the award.

How can vendors, providers, or consultants engage with Louisiana's program?

Eligibility runs on two tracks. **Direct-to-state contracts** go out as competitive NOFOs where firms bid as **prime contractors**. **Subgrants and subawards** flow to rural providers — hospitals, FQHCs/RHCs, ACOs and clinically integrated networks, EMS, and rural hospital-led partnerships. On the Medicaid APM, LDH is explicit that funding flows to Medicaid-enrolled provider entities and that vendors (it cites Equality Health and Azara as examples) can support implementation but are not the default recipients — the classic **subcontractor / implementation-partner** lane. The Rural Tech Catalyst Fund is a more directly vendor-facing vehicle.

Track opportunities on the [LA RHTP funding-opportunities page](https://ldh.la.gov/page/rhtp-funding-opportunities), watch for vendor showcases co-hosted with the Louisiana Rural Health Association, and monitor the [program hub](https://ldh.la.gov/page/rural-health-transformation-program).
