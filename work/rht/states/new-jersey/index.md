## Questions & answers

What is New Jersey's Rural Health Transformation Program allocation, and how is the funding structured?

New Jersey received **approximately $147.2 million** in Year 1 funding under the federal $50 billion Rural Health Transformation Program. As with every state, the award is disbursed by CMS annually across five years, with each year's payment tied to performance.

Which agency administers New Jersey's RHTP, and who leads it?

The [New Jersey Department of Health (NJDOH)](https://www.nj.gov/health/fhs/primarycare/rural-health/) administers the program through its Family Health Services / primary care office. Program contact: [RHTP.Application@doh.nj.gov](mailto:RHTP.Application@doh.nj.gov).

What is New Jersey's rural health transformation plan trying to accomplish?

New Jersey has published relatively little detail publicly so far. The visible thrust is expanding rural primary care and improving access for underserved populations, including migrant health access and rural maternal and child health — the state's first solicitation targets integrating community doulas into rural maternal care. For the fuller picture, watch the program hub.

[New Jersey RHTP hub →](https://www.nj.gov/health/fhs/primarycare/rural-health/)

Where is the money going — which initiatives is New Jersey funding, and at what share?

New Jersey has not published a public percentage-by-initiative breakdown yet. The state's tracked project priorities are migrant health access and rural primary care expansion, with an early spending signal in maternal and child health through its doula-integration RFA. The full program-by-program allocation is set out in New Jersey's approved budget narrative to CMS.

What has New Jersey procured so far, and what's open right now?

New Jersey's first published solicitation is an RFA for **Doula Integration in Rural New Jersey**, released April 10, 2026. NJDOH is seeking proposals to integrate NJ FamilyCare Community doulas into the maternal and child health landscape serving rural residents, with the goal of growing the size and capacity of the community doula workforce and supporting their engagement across health settings. The dispatch list below carries the running record as more opportunities post.

What are the key upcoming dates and deadlines in New Jersey?

No concrete forward deadlines are confirmed in the public record beyond the April 2026 doula RFA. New opportunities and their application windows surface first in the dated dispatches below and on the [NJ DOH Grant Programs Directory](https://healthapps.nj.gov/noticeofgrant/noticegrants.aspx).

What legislation or state action enables New Jersey's RHTP?

The Rural Health Transformation Program was created by **Section 71401 of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (2025)** and is administered federally by CMS. New Jersey administers its award through NJDOH under its CMS-approved application and budget; no separate enabling state statute is named in the record.

How can vendors, providers, or consultants engage with New Jersey's program?

Eligibility runs on two tracks. **Direct-to-state contracts** go out as competitive RFAs, where firms bid as **prime applicants**. **Subgrants and subawards** flow to rural providers — hospitals, Rural Health Clinics, FQHCs, maternal-health providers and community organizations — where technology vendors and consultants most often engage as **subcontractors or implementation partners**. New Jersey posts its opportunities through the NJ DOH grant notices directory.
