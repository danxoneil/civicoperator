## Questions & answers

What is Massachusetts's Rural Health Transformation Program allocation, and how is the funding structured?

Massachusetts is receiving approximately **$162.0 million** in Year-1 funding under the federal $50 billion Rural Health Transformation Program. Like every RHTP award, it is structured by CMS as an annual award across a five-year performance period. Detailed allocation figures live in the state's CMS-approved budget narrative; program updates are posted on the state's [RHTP hub](https://www.mass.gov/rural-health-transformation-program).

Which agency administers Massachusetts's RHTP, and who leads it?

Massachusetts's Rural Health Transformation Program is led by an **interagency team** rather than a single agency: the **Executive Office of Health and Human Services (EOHHS)**, the **Department of Public Health (DPH)**, **MassHealth** (the state Medicaid program), DPH's **Office of Rural Health**, and the Executive Office of Economic Development's **Office of Rural Affairs**, among other state agencies. Program contact: **Kirby Lecy** ([kirby.lecy@mass.gov](mailto:kirby.lecy@mass.gov)).

[Massachusetts RHTP overview →](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/rural-health-transformation-in-massachusetts)

What is Massachusetts's rural health transformation plan trying to accomplish?

Early signals point to a plan focused on rural workforce housing, pharmacy access, and tele-emergency care. Massachusetts has not yet published detailed public plan documents in the sources reviewed, so this profile is kept brief and pointing you to the source of record.

Where is the money going — which initiatives is Massachusetts funding, and at what share?

The key project areas signaled so far are **Workforce Housing**, **Pharmacy Access**, and **Tele-ED** (tele-emergency care). Specific dollar shares by initiative are not yet public in the primary sources reviewed; the full breakdown will appear in Massachusetts's budget narrative and on the [RHTP hub](https://www.mass.gov/rural-health-transformation-program).

Live dates will surface first in the dispatches below and on the [RHTP hub](https://www.mass.gov/rural-health-transformation-program); the Governor's office also posts related [news](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/governor-maura-healey-and-lt-governor-kim-driscoll/news).

What legislation or state action enables Massachusetts's RHTP?

The Rural Health Transformation Program was created by **Section 71401 of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (2025)** and is administered federally by CMS. Massachusetts administers its award under its CMS-approved project and budget narratives. No source confirms a Massachusetts-specific enabling statute or executive action from a primary source, and none is asserted here until it's documented.

How can vendors, providers, or consultants engage with Massachusetts's program?

Eligibility for RHTP funds generally runs on two tracks. **Direct-to-state contracts** go out as competitive RFPs, where firms bid as **prime contractors**. **Subgrants and subawards** flow to rural providers — hospitals, Rural Health Clinics, FQHCs, EMS agencies, pharmacies, and community organizations — where technology vendors and consultants most often engage as **subcontractors or implementation partners** rather than direct grantees. Massachusetts hasn't yet published its specific eligibility rules in the primary sources reviewed, so watch the hub for those details.
