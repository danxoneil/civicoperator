## Selected work

Connected systems that replaced a coalition's worth of spreadsheets, inboxes, and paper.

### A national public-health nonprofit

Built one connected directory of 4,600+ contacts and syndicated it as live, login-free views to 30+ member cities, each kept current by a self-service form. One system replaced a coalition's worth of spreadsheets.

### A regional behavioral-health support nonprofit

Turned a paper-and-inbox intake into one connected program: a family registers through a public form and the system creates their records, assigns an advisor, and opens an intake automatically — then tracks every client through to outcome against a 277-organization referral network.

### A major county health department

Gave field-based community health workers a simple way to file weekly reports that roll straight up into the executive dashboard metrics, with bilingual intake and rolling outcomes analysis.

### A multi-site sports-registration platform

Stabilized a fragile legacy platform end to end — modern hosting, a fixed core defect, a real deploy pipeline — so a business that runs on seasonal registration stopped fearing every release. Data pipelines and dashboards built on unlocked legacy systems.
